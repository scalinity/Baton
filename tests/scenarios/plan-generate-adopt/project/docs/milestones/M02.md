# M02 — Say what the note said

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M02. **Objective:** read what M01 recorded and say it back in the fixture's own words.
- **User-visible result:** `notes/said.txt` exists and repeats what `notes/read.txt` holds.
- **Size:** Small, about two hours; medium effort.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

M01 complete and merged, so `notes/read.txt` is on `main`.

## 3. Required reading

`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/MILESTONES.md`, this brief in full, and `docs/milestones/M01.md` for what M01
recorded and why.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

- **Scope:** read `notes/read.txt` and write `notes/said.txt`.
- **Non-goals:** re-reading the note itself; M01 owns that.

## 5. Expected files (proposed)

`notes/said.txt`, `docs/milestones/M02.md`.

## 6. Interfaces

- **Introduces:** `notes/said.txt`.
- **Consumes:** `notes/read.txt`.

## 7. Implementation checklist (ordered)

1. Read `notes/read.txt`.
2. Write `notes/said.txt` repeating it.
3. Verification, review and close-out.

## 8. Fixtures, verification, acceptance

- **Verification:** `test -s notes/said.txt`.
- **Acceptance:** `notes/said.txt` repeats what `notes/read.txt` holds.

## 9. Definition of done and evidence

The completing session appends what changed and the merge commit under Completion evidence.

## 10. Risks and decisions this milestone settles

- **`notes/read.txt` may say the note was empty.** Then `notes/said.txt` says so too, rather than
  being absent: absent and empty are different facts.

## 11. Handoff and next milestone

None. No milestone in `docs/MILESTONES.md` depends on M02, so its handover names no successor.

## Recovery procedure

Look at `notes/said.txt` and this brief's Completion evidence; resume the first unfinished item of
section 7 rather than starting over. No completion claim without section 8.
