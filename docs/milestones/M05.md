# M05 — Escalations, answer, allow, takeover

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M05. **Objective:** Everything that needs a person reaches them and comes back. Every
  escalation class parks its lane or project and is rendered as the three-part message on the Mac;
  `baton answer` delivers a ruling flagless under the label; `baton allow` widens in place; a
  taken-over lane is handed back. After this milestone a night can end in a park that a person
  resolves with one command in the morning.
- **User-visible result:** An `asking` artifact stops its session, parks the lane, notifies the
  Mac with the question, its numbered options and the marked recommendation, and
  `baton answer M0n 1` resumes the session with the option's text under the ruling label.
  `baton answer` with two parked lanes of the same milestone name refuses and prints
  `<project>/<milestone>` candidates. `baton status` shows the parked lane and the verb.

### 1. What changed

- **One writer for every escalation.** `escalate` in the new `lib/escalate.sh` replaces M03's
  `escalation_write`, and every M02–M04 writer calls it. It checks the class and the scope, attaches
  the hashes an edit is compared against, writes the event, and only then raises the Mac message, so
  D-057's "a message implies a record" holds for every class. `resolve` writes the three unparks and
  refuses a fourth.
- **The three-part message.** The address is the title; the body is the decision itself — a question
  with its options numbered and the recommendation marked, or the detail — then the verb that resolves
  that class, which is never what the length cap cuts. `status` prints the same verb (D-062).
- **The endings M04 routed and nothing acted on are parked.** `asking`, `merge-failed` and `other`
  park their lane at the consume, where the artifact is in hand. The `asking` carries is bounded in
  bytes and falls back to a pointer to the archive, so a stopped session is never left unparked
  (D-063). `main-broken` stays M06's.
- **`baton answer`** resolves a milestone across every project, acts on exactly one match, refuses
  several with the candidates and their one line, refuses a milestone that is not parked and an empty
  ruling, expands an option number from the archived artifact, and delivers the ruling label of §4.3.
  The resolution is written only once the resume was delivered or forked (D-061). On a lane taken over
  and not parked, `continue` is the hand-back: a resume with the continue template and no resolution
  (D-066).
- **`baton allow`** writes the rule as typed into the project's allowlist and the dispatched settings
  file in place, logs the widening, and refuses an ask rule — item 42 is unknown — JSON, an oversized
  or multi-line rule, and `--resume` on a parked lane (D-067).
- **A park ends when a person acts, and stays ended.** The tick re-reads the plan rows and the brief a
  park answers to and unparks on a change; a question answered in place unparks by its row or by the
  session's own later artifact. The rules that park a lane ask `person_acted` first: after an edit they
  redispatch, after a ruling they stand down until the session's next ending (D-060, D-061, D-065).
- **A resume waits for its stop to land** (D-064). Found by the live proof: the first ruling landed in
  a copy of the session.
- **One lane, one park.** The row check passes by a lane already parked, or dispatched or resumed
  within two intervals, so a lingering row can neither park a lane twice nor bring back a question a
  ruling answered (D-065).

### 6. Known limitations

- **`merge-failed` has no edit route when its session is gone.** REQ-STOP-12 names the plan edit as
  the fallback; M05 delivers the ruling half and M06 owns the rest.
- **A row reading `state: failed` with a live pid earns no class** (D-068): a model refused on the
  first turn still surfaces as the stall notification thirty minutes later.
- **A brief refreshed by another lane's close-out unparks an edit-class park on that milestone**, and
  now redispatches it. In one lane this cannot happen; with several it is a deliberate prompt update
  arriving, which is arguably right, and it is named here because it is not a person's direct edit.
- **A park for a session no ruling can reach** — a rejected artifact's lane Baton never dispatched, a
  hand-started session's question — ends only by an edit to the rows or the brief it answers to.
- **`prompt-lost` reads one listing.** It fires once per park, and the tick never proceeds on a
  listing it could not read.
- **A copy fork's row is not named `Baton · …`**, measured live. `copy_fork` resolves the copy by id,
  so nothing in Baton depends on the name; a person reading `claude agents` sees the CLI's own title.
- **`stop_settle` holds the lock** for up to sixty listings per resume while a stop fails to land.
- **Three probe sessions and one copy were real sessions** and spent real quota; all were stopped.
- **`lib/stops.sh` is past D-005's reversal condition, and its split is M06's.** The M04 review fixes
  that reached `main` during this milestone left a note assigning the declared-stops split to "the
  milestone that next opens this file". M05 opened it and did not split it: the note landed after
