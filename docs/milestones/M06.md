# M06 — The cap, the holds and the broken main

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M06. **Objective:** More than one lane, and the ground under all of them. The cap and
  its order; `fableReserve`; the plan winning on gates in both directions with `plan_override`;
  the two disagreement escalations; `main-broken` parking a project; leftover worktrees pruned on a
  verified merge. The first milestone that runs unattended overnight.
- **User-visible result:** With two fixture projects each holding an eligible milestone and the cap
  at two, both dispatch in the stated order and a third waits; a handover `held` on a cleared gate
  is dispatched with a `plan_override`; a plan-eligible milestone no handover lists escalates as
  `omitted`; a `main-broken` artifact holds every dispatch for that project while in-flight lanes
  run on; a worktree left behind by a `done` milestone with a verified `merged_as` is removed and
  logged, and one on a `merge-failed` lane is not.

## 10. Risks and decisions this milestone settles

  fixture; a `merge-failed` lane's worktree is the work.
- **The reserve is a proxy over an account-wide window.** Being an hour stale costs one Fable
  session that would have started anyway; do not add a per-model estimate.
- **A gate is the person's recorded act and the newer word**; the plan wins in both directions and
  the override is logged, never escalated.
