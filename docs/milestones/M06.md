# M06 — The cap, the holds and the broken main

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M06. **Objective:** More than one lane, and the ground under all of them. The cap and
  its order; `fableReserve`; the plan winning on gates in both directions with `plan_override`;
  the two disagreement escalations; `main-broken` parking a project; leftover worktrees pruned on a
  verified merge. The first milestone that runs unattended overnight.
