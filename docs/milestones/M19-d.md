# M19-d — Status shows a paused lane

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M19-d. **Objective:** `baton status` names every paused lane and the stand-down.
- **User-visible result:** `baton status` prints one line per paused lane, with when it was paused,
  whether by one-lane pause or the stand-down, and the verb that resumes it.
- **Size:** Small, about 1 hour; medium effort. M19-c depends on the same milestone and touches none
  of this milestone's files; the two may run together.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

M19-b complete and installed: `derive_in_flight` returns the `paused` list this milestone prints,
and the `pause` events carry `scope`. Read M19-b's completion evidence for their actual shape.

`lib/render.sh`.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**Why.** `status_render` prints only lanes with a live row, deliberately: `no_row` holds sessions
Baton stopped on an asking consume or that ended with a declared stop, and printing those as in
flight would show a parked lane twice (`lib/status.sh:264–267`). A consequence observed in this
project: from 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-01 the stopped lanes of M15-c and M15-d were open and appeared
nowhere in `baton status`. M19-b moves a paused lane out of `no_row` into `paused`, so without this
milestone a paused lane is invisible too, while the stand-down shows only as its hold line.

**Scope.** One status section, after the in-flight lanes: per paused lane, project and milestone,
attempt, short session id, paused at, `lane` or `stand-down`, and `baton resume <milestone>`; when a
stand-down holds, one header line saying so and `baton resume` with no argument. Every line goes
through `lib/render.sh` and stays matchable when piped and under `NO_COLOR` (REQ-VERB-09). The
existing hold line for the `pause` cause is kept or replaced by the header, not printed twice.

**Non-goals.** No change to how a rowless, unpaused lane is shown: the comment's reasons stand, and
M19 makes such a lane reach a person through the ladder's park instead. No change to the cap, the
dispatch refusal (M19-c) or any derivation.
