# M24-b — A verb waits a moment for the tick instead of refusing

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M24-b. **Objective:** a person's verb that finds the lock held by a live tick waits for it,
  within a bound, instead of doing nothing.
- **User-visible result:** `baton answer`, `baton dispatch` and the other verbs run when typed, even
  in the seconds a tick holds the lock; only a holder that outlasts the bound is refused, as today.
- **Size:** Small, about 1 hour; medium effort.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

M23 complete and installed: it changes `lib/lock.sh`, the file this milestone edits. Read its
completion evidence for how a release is recorded.

## 3. Required reading

`CLAUDE.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `CONTRACT.md`; M21's and M23's completion evidence; `docs/DECISIONS.md`
D-174; `docs/SPEC.md` on the lock. Code, at the merged `main`: `lib/lock.sh` `lock_take` (the
`lock held … nothing done` refusal, around line 14 of its body); `bin/baton`'s `lock_take` calls per
verb; `lib/tick.sh`'s own lock use.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**The measured finding.** On 2026-10-01 at 22:38:48 `baton answer M15-e "<ruling>"` printed
`baton: lock held at /Users/danny/.baton/lock (pid 17242, since 2026-10-01T22:38:38-04:00); nothing
done`. The holder was a tick, which finished at 22:38:43; the person had to type the command again.
A tick normally finishes in seconds and, under load, in about seventy.

**Scope.**

1. **A verb waits; the tick does not.** When a verb, not the tick, finds the lock held by a live
   holder (one that answers `kill -0`), it says once that it is waiting for the holder and polls
   until the lock is free or a bound passes (settle it from M21's measured tick length, about two
   minutes), then takes the lock and runs. Past the bound it refuses exactly as today. The tick keeps
   its immediate "nothing done": launchd starts another in a minute.
2. **A stale lock is unchanged.** A holder that does not answer is cleared and reported as M21 left
   it.

**Non-goals.** No queue of verbs, no change to the lock's format, no change to how the tick takes it.
