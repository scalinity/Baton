# M15-f — The scope guard holds every route to a successor

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M15-f. **Objective:** no successor of a milestone starts while that milestone's scope
  check is owed or running, whichever route would start it.
- **User-visible result:** a successor waits for its dependency's scope check even in the minute
  between the dependency writing `done` and Baton consuming its handover, and even when it was
  parked `blocked` on that dependency.
- **Size:** Small to Medium, about 2 hours; high effort.

never run at once. M15-c's guard (`lib/scope.sh`) is on `main`. Read both completion evidences.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**The finding.** A review on 2026-10-01 (finding R06), confirmed by reading the code, found two routes
that start a successor without the guard:

1. **The close-out window.** `scope_hold` holds a candidate only for a dependency that has a
   `consumed` `complete` carrying a scope request. A close-out writes `done` into the plan at
   `CONTRACT.md` clause 3(c) and its artifact at (d); a tick between them sees the dependency done,
   turns a seed's or an older handover's `wait` on it into a candidate (`lib/candidates.sh`, the
   `wait` arm; the mid-run check suspends only `omitted`), and `scope_hold` finds no consumed event
   to hold on. On 2026-10-01 M15-c sat in that window from its `done` commit `a3a56797` until its
   handover was rejected at 17:11:31.
2. **The blocked redispatch.** A lane parked `blocked` on a dependency is redispatched by
   `declared_step` as soon as the dependency reads `done` (`lib/declared.sh:121`), through
   `redispatch`, which never asks `scope_hold`.

**Scope.**

1. `scope_hold` also holds a candidate whose dependency reads `done` in the plan but has no
   `consumed` `complete` since its newest dispatch: the close-out is under way and its scope request
   cannot exist yet. The hold reads "waits for the dependency's close-out", in the same table
   `scope_hold` already renders.
2. The `blocked` arm of `declared_step` asks the same hold before it redispatches; a held lane stays
   parked and is redispatched on the tick the hold lifts.
3. Neither change adds a model call, an event kind, or a second admission rule: the hold is the
   guard's, read where a successor would start.

**Non-goals.** No change to the guard's judgment, to its own admission, or to `omitted`. No change to
the ladder's `redispatch` for a lane's own failures, which has no dependency to wait for.

## 8. Fixtures, verification, acceptance

Full-tick fixtures, a fixed clock and the `claude` shim; run each twice and `sh tests/run.sh`.

- A parent's row reads `done`, its artifact not yet in the inbox, a seed `wait` on it for the
  successor: no `dispatch` of the successor; after the artifact is consumed and the guard passes, one.
- A successor parked `blocked` on the parent, the parent reads `done`, its guard pending: no
  `redispatch`; after the guard passes, one.
- The guard's own lane is admitted throughout, with the cap full of other work as D-205 settles.

## 9. Definition of done and evidence

The handoff template in `docs/MILESTONES.md`; the event sequences; `sh tests/run.sh`'s scenario and
failure counts, read to completion. An unrun check is never reported as passed.

## 10. Risks and decisions this milestone settles

A hold that never lifts is the failure to fear: a dependency whose close-out never produces a consumed
`complete` (rejected, stopped) must still reach a person through its own park, and the held successor
must say what it waits for. Record the decision at the next free D-number read from `main` when it is
written. Apply M19 §10, "Main advances while a session runs", in full.
