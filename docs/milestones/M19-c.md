# M19-c — A paused lane keeps its place

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M19-c. **Objective:** a paused lane holds its slot under the cap, and no dispatch opens a
  new attempt over it.
- **User-visible result:** with the cap at two, one lane paused and one running, a third eligible
  milestone waits; `baton dispatch` of a paused milestone is refused and names `baton resume`.
- **Size:** Small, about 1–2 hours; medium effort. M19-d (status) depends on the same milestone and
  touches none of this milestone's files; the two may run together.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**Why.** The cap is the Mac's, across every registered project (`lib/tick.sh`: "The cap is the
Mac's, so no project has a share of it"). `dispatch_run` counts it as `.in_flight | length` and per
project from the same list, so a lane whose process has stopped frees its slot. A pause taken to save
usage would then let the tick start other work in its place, spending what the pause was meant to
save. Separately, `verb_dispatch` refuses only when a live row carries the milestone's name; on
2026-10-01 a hand `baton dispatch` opened attempt 2 of M15-c and M15-d over their open, rowless
attempt 1. Over a paused lane that would strand the paused session on an attempt nothing tracks.

**Scope.**

1. **The cap counts paused lanes.** `dispatch_run`'s total and its per-project counts include every
   paused lane, each counted once by project, milestone and attempt, beside the live ones. Nothing
   pretends a paused lane has a pid: `in_flight` keeps meaning a live process, because the watchers,
   the keep-awake derivation and planning read it as that.
2. **No dispatch over a paused lane.** `verb_dispatch` refuses a milestone that has a paused lane,
   writes nothing, and names `baton resume <milestone>`. If M19-b found that the tick's dispatch
   pass can pick a milestone with an open paused lane, the same refusal guards `dispatch_one`; if it
   found it cannot, a fixture keeps proving it.

**Non-goals.** No per-project cap (the cap stays the Mac's), no change to what counts as live, no
change to the budget room, which counts starts and not occupancy. `baton status` listing paused
lanes is M19-d's.
