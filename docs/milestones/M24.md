# M24 — A hand dispatch clears its own omitted park; a stray key is not a "no"

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M24. **Objective:** two rough edges a person met on 2026-10-01: a milestone started by hand
  stays parked `omitted`, and onboarding reads an arrow key typed while it thinks as a refusal.
- **User-visible result:** `baton dispatch` of a milestone no handover lists leaves no `omitted` park
  behind; answering `yes` to onboarding registers the project even if a key was pressed while the
  summary was being written.
- **Size:** Small, about 1–2 hours; medium effort.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

None in the plan. `lib/dispatch.sh` and `lib/onboard.sh` are edited by no milestone in flight; M19-c
edits `lib/dispatch.sh` later and follows its own chain.

## 3. Required reading

`CLAUDE.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `CONTRACT.md`; `docs/SPEC.md` REQ-DISPATCH-01/02, REQ-ESC (the `omitted`
class), REQ-ONBOARD-06; `docs/DECISIONS.md` D-102, D-156. Code, at the merged `main`:

`onboard_confirm` (around 590–600).

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**The measured finding.**

- On 2026-10-01 M20, M21 and M22 were added to the plan by hand and started with `baton dispatch`.
  Each was parked `omitted` ("no archived handover of Baton lists M…, though the plan makes it
  eligible"), M20's at 17:27:37 and M22's still standing at 22:51 with its session running. A park
  stands its lane by (`stops_standing_by`), so the running lane got no crash recovery or wait retry
  until a later plan edit cleared it ("the work plan it answers to changed").
- On the same night a person answered `yes` to `baton onboard` and was told "not confirmed, so Baton
  is not registered". The terminal showed `^[[B` before the summary: a down-arrow pressed while the
  judgment ran reached stdin ahead of the answer, so `read -r oc_answer` read `␛[Byes`, which matches
  none of `yes|Yes|YES|y|Y`. The second run, with no key pressed, registered Baton.

**Scope.**

1. **A hand dispatch answers the omitted park.** When `verb_dispatch` starts a milestone that has an
   open `omitted` park, it writes that park's `resolution` (reason: dispatched by hand) under the
   lock, through `resolve`. No other park is touched; `disagreement` is not `omitted`.
2. **Terminal noise is not an answer.** `onboard_confirm` removes ANSI escape sequences and control
   characters and trims surrounding whitespace from the line it reads, then compares exactly as now.
   A typo stays a no: `yse`, `ye` and `yes please` are still refused.

**Non-goals.** No change to when `omitted` is raised, to the confirmation's wording or length, or to
what counts as assent.
