# M04 — Waits, the ladder and continuations

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M04. **Objective:** Every ending is acted on by its rule. An API error is waited out
  and resumed; a turn that ended without a handover climbs the ladder; a crash confirmed in M03 is
  resumed or redispatched; a copy fork is recorded and the attempt follows the new id; the
  per-model hold bites. The first milestone dispatched by a tick.
- **User-visible result:** A fixture `api-error` artifact produces a `wait_retry` every fifteen
  minutes of fixture time, a `hold` on its model, a notification at the ceiling, and a flagless
  resume with the continue template when the shim lets it through. A `no-handover` produces one
  resume with the finish template, then a redispatch as attempt 2, then a `ladder-end` escalation.

handover. The person is watching: the ladder this milestone builds does not yet exist while it

### 1. What changed

- **Step 4 has a body.** `stops_run` in `lib/stops.sh` runs between the row checks and the caffeinate
  re-arm and acts on every ending in the order the facts arrive: the waits and their retries, the
  ladder, the declared stops, the distant wait. A crash step 3 confirmed a moment earlier is an
  ending this step acts on in the same tick, and a resume it issues is a fact the ladder then reads.
- **`route_ending` is the taxonomy as data**, one lookup from `(outcome, reason, error)` to an
  action, a retry bit, a notify bit and whether the class holds its model. Every rule reads that
  answer rather than testing the error again, which is what makes a wrong label cost one refused
  request per interval and never a night. An error value the table does not know lands where the
  five named unrecoverable ones land — notify now, keep retrying — because that is the safe
  direction for a value the CLI adds tomorrow.
- **The wait** is a stop-then-flagless-resume every `retryMinutes` that spends no attempt and never
  redispatches. Its clock is `wait_run`: the *oldest* `api-error` consume since the attempt's reset,
  not the newest, so a standing limit that writes a fresh artifact every cycle does not reset the
  elapsed time and the ceilings arrive (D-052). A ceiling notifies once per class per attempt
  through derivation 11 and never stops the retries.
- **The hold** is written by `holds_apply`, once per tick before any project's step 4, because a
  usage limit is a fact about the account and not about a lane. A second limited model writes a
  `hold` with `model: all`; all of them lift when the waits clear, not when a resume is delivered.
  `hold_bites` is read before every dispatch this milestone can cause — the tick's step 7 and the
  redispatch alike — which is what makes REQ-STOP-13 a hold rather than a record of one (D-055).
- **`resume_session` is the one way Baton speaks to a running session**: `claude stop`, then a
  flagless `claude --bg --resume <uuid> "<text>"`, then a classification over *both* streams with the
  fork test before the success test. A fork stops the original again and writes `copy_fork` carrying
  the session id whose prefix the note's job id is (D-051).
- **The ladder** is `ladder_position` — derivation 9 plus the newest ending and whether a step has
  been taken for it — and `ladder_step`. A lane with an active wait is the wait's and not the
  ladder's, so the two cannot resume one session twice in a tick; the escalate rung still fires,
  because three refused resumes mean the session cannot be resumed at all and the lane is better
  handed over than retried until morning.
- **The declared stops**: `unfinished` redispatches and a second consecutive one escalates with both
  splits; `blocked` is silent while its blocker is in flight or eligible, redispatches when the plan
  reads the blocker `done`, and escalates when nothing is coming to unblock it; `invalid_request`
  redispatches without ever resuming, and a second consecutive one ends the ladder (D-053).
- **The crash rule gained one guard**: a lane dispatched or resumed within two intervals is starting
  up, not crashed (D-054). Nothing needed it until something acted on a confirmed crash; without it
  the tick sights the session it has just resumed and the ladder counts its own remedy.
- **`cli_plain`**: everything Baton reads from the CLI's own output has the colour taken out of it
  first (D-050), which is what makes a hand-run dispatch from inside a session work at all.
- **A message implies a record**: neither writer in `lib/notify.sh` raises the Mac message when
  `log_event` refused the line, and `artifact_detail` bounds the one field a session sizes (D-057).

  two live sessions on one branch, and the ladder climbing on a resume that in fact succeeded —
  precisely what the classifier exists to prevent. Neither reviewer could confirm the CLI emits it
  today, and item 46 captured a different one, but a rule that reads seven of eight is not a rule.
  The test now matches the family and the id is the token after the last ` as ` (D-058), with the
  fixture `copy-fork-generic-note`. §4.3's rule is corrected to match.

Corroborated by the auditor and a debugger, and fixed:

- **The `copy_fork` event is now written before the second `claude stop`.** The two cannot be
  atomic; the order is chosen for what a recovery reads, because until the event exists
  `current_session` still answers with the original (D-059).
- **`holds_apply` was the one step in the tick called with no guard.** `tick_run` is invoked as

### 6. Known limitations

- **A model the plan names and the API refuses does not reach the `model_not_found` routing**
  (D-056). Item 44 measured that such a session is created, makes one refused request, and ends with
  `state: failed`, a live pid and **no artifact**. The tick reads a transcript's modification time
  and never its content (REQ-STOP-01), so the `error` the transcript carries is not available to it,
  and `state: failed` with a live pid is not a class the fixed taxonomy has. The lane is not silent:
  thirty minutes of an unchanging transcript notifies a stall naming the row's state. Closing it
  properly means a new class, which belongs to the milestone that owns them.
- **`merge-failed`, `main-broken`, `other` and `asking` are routed by the table and acted on by
  nothing.** `route_ending` names each; the escalation classes they need are M05's and M06's. A
  session that writes one of those reasons is consumed and recorded, and the person reads it from
  `baton status`'s inbox line and the archive.
- **An `invalid_request` redispatch starts a new attempt whose ladder is empty**, because derivation
  9 keys on the attempt and a dispatch is its reset point. REQ-STOP-02's "the next failure ending
  escalates" is therefore delivered by the consecutive-`invalid_request` bound rather than by the
  ladder's count (D-053).
- **`derive_waits` reports a wait for an `invalid_request` or a `model_not_found` too**, because it
  selects on the reason and not the error, so `baton status` prints such a lane as waiting until the
  redispatch or the park resolves it. The routing is right; the view is a milestone behind.
