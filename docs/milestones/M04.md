# M04 — Waits, the ladder and continuations

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M04. **Objective:** Every ending is acted on by its rule. An API error is waited out
  and resumed; a turn that ended without a handover climbs the ladder; a crash confirmed in M03 is
  resumed or redispatched; a copy fork is recorded and the attempt follows the new id; the
  per-model hold bites. The first milestone dispatched by a tick.
- **User-visible result:** A fixture `api-error` artifact produces a `wait_retry` every fifteen
  minutes of fixture time, a `hold` on its model, a notification at the ceiling, and a flagless
  resume with the continue template when the shim lets it through. A `no-handover` produces one
  resume with the finish template, then a redispatch as attempt 2, then a `ladder-end` escalation.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

M03 complete, installed and the plist loaded; this milestone is dispatched by the tick from M03's
handover. The person is watching: the ladder this milestone builds does not yet exist while it
runs, so a stopped session is theirs to notice from `baton status` and `Claude.app`.

## 7. Implementation checklist (ordered)

1. `route_ending` as one table from `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.2: `(outcome, reason, error)` →
   action, retry bit, notify bit.
2. The wait: `wait_due` from derivation 5; `wait_retry_run` does `claude stop`, flagless resume
   with the continue template, `wait_retry` event with `since` and `retry`; the ceilings via
   derivation 11; `hold` on first consume, `hold_lifted` on a delivered resume; `caffeinate -i -t`
   for the remainder of `caffeinateMaxHours`.
3. The hold's second-model rule: a `rate_limit` on another model while one is held → `hold`
   with `model: all`; both lift together.
4. `resume_session`: stop, flagless resume, stdout and stderr captured; parse `woke session` for
   `delivered`, the `note:` variants for `forked` (the new id after `started a copy as `), anything
   else for `refused`; write the events; on `forked`, `claude stop` the original again and log.
5. The ladder: derivation 9 → resume with finish (after `no-handover`) or continue (after a
   crash), then `redispatch` via `dispatch_one` (attempt n+1, the slot line's attempt sentence
   naming the branch's commit), then `escalation` class `ladder-end` with the last artifact's
   `detail` or the crash sighting.
6. `invalid_request` → redispatch, never resume; `model_not_found` → `escalation` at once, no
   retry, message naming the cell to edit; `unfinished` → redispatch, the second consecutive →
   `escalation` with both splits; `blocked` → silent while the blocker is in flight or eligible
   (notification `blocked_by` once), redispatch when it reads `done`, else `escalation`;
   `distant_wait_for` once.
7. The fixtures in §5, twice each; the shim gains `note:` and `woke session` modes.
8. Live, once, in a fixture project: item 46 (a forced copy fork's exact `note:` line and stream)
   and item 44 (`--model` with a refused alias → `model_not_found` artifact). Item 18 cannot be
   forced; record it unrun unless a real limit occurs during the session.
9. `sh tests/run.sh`, `/review-2`, `/address`, completion evidence, the handover.

**Natural split point after item 5.** The wait and the ladder exist; the declared-stop routing
becomes `M04-b`.

## 10. Risks and decisions this milestone settles

- **The `note:` line's stream (item 46).** Capture both streams; the parse rule survives every
  variant but the classification `refused` must not swallow a fork that went to the other stream.
- **A wait resumes into the same context.** The continue template says "not a fault in the work"
  because the model otherwise works around a limit; keep that sentence.
- **The redispatch's worktree is the reused one**, so attempt 2 resumes on the branch that holds
  the evidence; the slot line must name its commit.
- **`caffeinate -i -t` renewals stretch through sleep**; the bound is measured from `since`, not
  counted in renewals.
