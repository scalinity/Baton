# M19-e — Stop in Claude.app pauses the lane

## 1. Identity

- **ID:** M19-e. **Objective:** pressing Stop on a Baton session in Claude.app, or on the phone
  through Remote Control, pauses that lane as `baton pause <milestone>` would, with no terminal.
- **User-visible result:** a lane stopped from Claude.app is shown paused, not stalled, never
  sighted as a crash, and holds its place; typing to the session in Claude.app or on the phone
  continues it, and `baton resume <milestone>` does too.
- **Size:** Small to Medium, about 2–3 hours including one live measurement; high effort.

## 2. Dependencies and entry conditions

M19-b complete and installed: this milestone writes M19-b's `pause` event and relies on its
derivation and on its rule that a pause ends when the transcript shows the session working again.
Read M19-b's completion evidence for the actual names. M19-c (the slot) and M19-d (status) need not
be done; a lane this milestone pauses gains both when they land.

## 4. Requirements, scope, non-goals

**The measured finding.** Pressing Stop on a running Baton session interrupts its turn; it does not
end the process. Evidence from this project's own record:

- M15-c, session `7f9e3517`, dispatched 2026-09-24 12:08:04 local. Its transcript's last
  conversational entry is a `user` entry whose text is `[Request interrupted by user]`, at
  16:08:57Z (12:08:57 local), with nothing after. M15-d, `a716bde4`, dispatched 12:08:06, has the
  same entry at 16:09:03Z, nothing after.
- The processes lived on. At 12:39:32 the tick notified a stall for both, "no transcript change for
  30 m; the row reads working"; at 18:08:13 it notified both as running six hours. Only at 23:07:53
  were both rows gone (`crash_sighting` 1), and what ended the two processes then is not measured:
  `~/Library/Logs/Claude/main.log` holds no entry between 23:02 and 23:09.
- M07, session `e7f442dc`, dispatched 2026-09-12 16:49:32 local: the marker one minute later
  (20:50:30Z), the row gone by 16:57:29, Baton's crash resume delivered at 16:58:31, a second marker
  21 seconds after that, and the person's own message 808 seconds later. M17, `13f60e15`: the marker
  at 2026-09-18 02:33:22Z with nothing after.
- Across the 75 transcripts of Baton-dispatched sessions, every exact marker entry followed a
  person's Stop; none preceded a "Baton resumed this session" message. Baton's log holds five
  resumes (two rulings delivered, one ruling forked, one continue delivered, one continue forked).
  Whether `claude stop <job>` itself writes the marker is not measured, and that sample is too small
  to stand in for the measurement.
- The variant `[Request interrupted by user for tool use]` occurred once (M05), with the person's
  next message in the same second: an interruption to redirect, not to stop.

So Baton already sees the person's intent, in the transcript, and read it as a stall, then a
long-running lane, then, once the processes went, a crash it could not recover.

**Scope.**

1. **Measure first.** On a fixture project this milestone creates at `~/.baton/m19e-probe/`, start
   one `--bg` session named `Baton · M19-e probe` and record, verbatim, into
   `~/.baton/m19e-probe/README.md`: (a) after the person presses Stop on it in Claude.app — asked
   for in the session with `AskUserQuestion`, because only a person can press it — the transcript's
   newest entries, the row's `state` and `pid`; (b) after `claude stop <job>` on it, the same; (c)
   after a flagless `claude --bg --resume <uuid> "<text>"`, the same. That session is the only real
   session this milestone may start, stop or resume. If (b) writes the marker, a marker within the
   window of a stop Baton logged for that session is not a person's, and §8 gains that case.
2. **Read the Stop as a pause.** In `stops_run`, for each open lane that is not paused, parked or
   taken over, read the newest `user` or `assistant` entry of the current session's transcript. If
   it is a `user` entry whose text begins `[Request interrupted by user` and it is at least one tick
   interval old, write M19-b's `pause {session, scope: "lane", by: "interrupt", marker_at}` once.
   The interval is the grace for an interruption to redirect: a person who presses Stop and types at
   once never produces a pause. From then on the lane is paused in every respect M19-b defines.
3. **The order already works.** `crash_check` runs before `stops_run`. A session interrupted and then
   gone before Baton saw the marker gets at most a first sighting, which expires; the pause written
   in the same tick keeps it out of `no_row`, so no second sighting and no ladder step follow.
4. **Continuing needs no terminal while the process lives.** Typing to the session in Claude.app or
   on the phone writes a newer entry, and M19-b ends the pause. When the process has gone, as both
   M15 sessions' had by 23:07, a message typed in Claude.app is held until the session is resumed
   (SPEC's setup facts), so `baton resume <milestone>` is the way back; M19-d's status line names it.
5. **Nothing is sent.** The person paused the lane; there is no Mac message for it. `baton status`
   shows it (M19-d).

**Non-goals.** No reading of intent from a row disappearing, from the state field, or from anything
but the marker. No change to `crash_check`. No automatic resume of a lane a person stopped. No
remote resume of a session whose process has gone.

## 8. Fixtures, verification, acceptance

Fixtures use the `claude` shim, a fixed clock and fixture transcripts; only §4 item 1 touches a real
session. Run each scenario twice and `sh tests/run.sh`. Assert event contents and counts.

- The marker newest and older than one interval: one `pause {by: interrupt}`; with the clock past
  `stallMinutes` and `longRunningHours`, no stall and no long-running notification over ten ticks,
  beside an unpaused positive control that is notified.
- The marker followed within the interval by a `user` entry: no `pause`.
- The marker, then the row gone before the next tick: at most one `crash_sighting` (sighting 1), one
  `pause`, no sighting 2, no `resume`, `redispatch` or `rung_refused`.
- A paused-by-interrupt lane whose transcript gains a newer entry: one `pause_ended`, then in flight.
- A parked or taken-over lane whose transcript ends in the marker: no `pause`.
- The replay: dispatch at 12:08:04, the marker at 12:08:57, ticks every sixty seconds to 23:08:58
  with the rows live and idle until 23:07: one `pause` by 12:10:57, no stall, no long-running, no
  crash sighting after it.

## 10. Risks and decisions this milestone settles

The marker is Claude Code's text, not a contract: the measurement records the version it was taken
on, and a change to the text makes Baton fall back to today's reading (stall, then the crash path
M19 made recoverable), never to a false pause. Record the decision at the next free D-number read
from `main` when it is written. Apply M19 §10, "Main advances while a session runs", in full.
